# Skill: Retained Counsel Package & Conflict Check Roster

## Purpose
Prepares factual consultation dossiers and conflict-check summaries for licensed qui tam attorneys when insider records are held.

## Conflict-Check Roster (`whip conflict-roster`)
Before an attorney can review a case narrative, law firms must run an internal conflict-of-interest check to ensure they do not already represent the target entity or related parties.

The Conflict-Check Roster includes:
- Target Entity Legal Name
- CMS NPI (if applicable)
- Practice Address
- Governing State
- **ZERO factual narrative or allegations.**
- Notice: "Provided strictly to facilitate attorney conflict-of-interest screening pursuant to professional conduct rules."

## Counsel Factual Record Index
When consulting licensed False Claims Act counsel:
- All documents remain encrypted in the local airgapped vault.
- Compiles an objective index of available exhibits, dates, and full SHA-256 digests.
- Uses the verified insider consultation footer:
  > "This factual record index was compiled offline by the client for legal consultation. Just Liberty Incorporated is not legal counsel. This document is provided to retained counsel to facilitate legal evaluation."
- Never attaches unsworn 28 U.S.C. § 1746 perjury declarations to unsworn informal drafts.
