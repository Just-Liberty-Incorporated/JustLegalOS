# The 14-Pathway Systematic Accountability Map

When examining state agency or contractor misconduct, evaluate all 14 statutory pathways using:
```bash
justlegalos-cli accountability map <case-folder>
```

## The 14 Statutory Remedies
1. **State Administrative Appeal:** Internal agency appeal (e.g. hearing officer; strict statutory deadlines, typically 20 days).
2. **Industrial Claim Appeals Board:** Review of intermediate administrative rulings.
3. **State Court of Appeals:** Direct judicial review of final agency orders.
4. **Federal Program OIG Hotline:** Direct referral to federal oversight bodies (DOL-OIG, HHS-OIG, USDA-OIG).
5. **State Auditor Fraud Hotline:** Independent legislative audit evaluation for systemic fiscal waste.
6. **State Attorney General / MFCU:** State False Claims Act enforcement against state contractors.
7. **Federal False Claims Act (Qui Tam):** Treble damages against private contractors under 31 U.S.C. § 3729.
8. **Statutory Public Records (CORA / FOIA):** Discovery of agency vendor contracts, defect logs, and invoices.
9. **Claimant Own-Record Inspection:** Access to individual claim files free of charge under 20 CFR § 603.5(d).
10. **Federal Civil Rights (§ 1983):** Prospective injunctive relief under *Ex parte Young*, 209 U.S. 123 (1908).
11. **State APA Rule Challenge:** Challenging unpromulgated or arbitrary agency policies.
12. **Agency Customer Service Escalation:** Internal executive advocacy escalation.
13. **Legislative Constituent Inquiry:** State representative or senator casework request.
14. **Bar / Ethics Grievance:** Grievance for prosecutorial or attorney misconduct (requires non-tolling advisory).

## Viability Ratings
- **Solid:** Strong statutory basis and clear timeline.
- **Fixable:** Viable theory, but missing prerequisite evidence (e.g., needs contractor invoice).
- **Long shot:** High legal barrier (qualified immunity or discretionary audit).
- **Dead end:** Legally barred (e.g., Qui Tam against state agency under *Stevens*).
