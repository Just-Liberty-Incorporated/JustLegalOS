# Independent Forensic Analysis Standard (DOJ Directive 26-12)

Pursuant to DOJ Directive 26-12 and 28 U.S.C. § 530C(b)(1)(L), the Fraud Whistleblower Incentive Program (WHIP on america.gov/fraud) awards $10,000 to $30,000 for tips based on "Independent Analysis" of publicly available information.

## What Constitutes Qualifying Independent Analysis
DOJ defines Independent Analysis as:
> "The individual's own analysis, whether done alone or in combination with others. Analysis means the individual's examination and evaluation of information that may be publicly available, but which reveals information that is not generally known or available to the public."

A raw copy-paste of a public website does not qualify. The citizen must synthesize public registries against observed physical reality to expose an undisclosed discrepancy.

## The Standardized Section II Block
When `justlegalos-cli whip prepare <case>` is run, it generates this exact structured block:

```text
======================================================================
SECTION II: INDEPENDENT FORENSIC ANALYSIS (PURSUANT TO DIRECTIVE 26-12)
======================================================================
1. Public Registry Verification: Target Entity [Name] is registered in CMS
   NPPES under NPI [10-Digit] with primary Healthcare Provider Taxonomy
   [Code - Description]. Authorized official: [Name].
2. Federal Program Funding Nexus: Federal grant/Medicaid drawdown data
   from USAspending.gov confirms $[Amount] in obligations under Assistance
   Listing Number (ALN) [Code - Description] for the trailing 12 months.
3. Observed Physical Reality: Multi-day timestamped forensic photo logging
   conducted on [Dates] during mandatory posted operating hours ([Hours])
   confirmed zero client/patient traffic, locked entrances, and dark interior.
   Photographic evidence cryptographically secured with SHA-256 hashes.
4. Forensic Discrepancy & Materiality: Operating footprint contradicts the
   facility capacity, staffing ratios, and service volume legally required
   to support $[Amount] in taxpayer disbursements.
======================================================================
```

Always include this block in final submission text prepared for america.gov/fraud.
