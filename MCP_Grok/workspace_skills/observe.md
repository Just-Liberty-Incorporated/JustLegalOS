# Skill: Citizen Field Observation & Recording Rules

## Purpose
Provides objective, lawful guidelines for citizens documenting exterior facilities and service providers from public vantage points.

## Universal 6 Rules for Citizen Observers
1. **Public Property Only**:
   - Remain strictly on public sidewalks, roadways, or open exterior commercial lots. Never enter private property or restricted areas without permission.
2. **No Criminal Trespass**:
   - Never pick locks, touch locked door handles, climb fences, or peer through closed window blinds on private premises.
3. **DO NOT Record Audio**:
   - Audio wiretap recording consent laws vary significantly across states (with 12 all-party consent jurisdictions). Recording workplace or healthcare conversations creates severe criminal and civil exposure. Rely strictly on visual observation of exterior signage and posted hours.
4. **No Staff or Patient Confrontation**:
   - Do not accost, interrogate, or confront employees, visitors, or patients. Observations must be entirely passive.
5. **No Social Media Publishing**:
   - Never post observation photos, videos, or worker names to social media (X, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit). Public posting can destroy statutory reward eligibility, trigger defamation/privacy torts, and alert targets to alter records.
6. **Objective Factual Logging**:
   - Record exact calendar dates, times, and physical conditions (e.g. "Front door locked during posted operating hours"). Never invent accusations or state legal conclusions.

## DOJ Pilot Program Standards
- **Endnote 2**: "Information provided to the Department must be truthful and complete. The Department does not authorize or condone any unlawful conduct in the gathering of information."
